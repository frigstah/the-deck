The Deck

A live radio broadcaster for Windows, built as one screen. Icecast and SHOUTcast,
free and unrestricted: no paid tier, no bitrate cap, no locked features.


THIS IS AN ALPHA BUILD
----------------------
Built automatically from the latest commit. It has not been through a release
check of any kind. Expect rough edges, and please do not use it for a show that
matters until you have run it once yourself and heard the result.


RUNNING IT
----------
Run Deck.exe. There is no .NET download to do first - everything needed is in
this folder, which is why it is so large.

Windows may warn that it does not recognise the program, because these builds
are not code-signed. Click "More info" then "Run anyway" if you are happy to.

Settings live in:
    %APPDATA%\Deck
unless the file deck-portable.txt sits next to Deck.exe, in which case they go
in a "data" folder here instead. The portable download has that file already;
the installer does not.


THE WINDOW
----------
The Deck opens on the deck: whether you are on air, for how long, the meter,
what you sound like, where it is going, and what listeners are seeing. That is
the whole screen and it holds no settings at all.

Everything you can change is behind the Setup button, as a panel that covers the
deck. Press "Deck" in the bottom-left of that panel to come back. While setup is
open a strip along the bottom keeps showing whether you are live, so you never
lose sight of it.

The window is deliberately short. It is meant to sit along the edge of a screen
while something else plays the music.


THE FIRST THREE MINUTES
-----------------------
This is the part worth your attention, because it is the whole point:

  1. Setup, Sound    - pick your input, watch the level say "Sounds good",
                       press Sound Check to hear yourself as listeners will.
  2. Setup, Servers  - paste the stream URL your host gave you. The Deck fills
                       in the rest. Press Test.
  3. Back to the deck and press Go live.

If any of that took longer than three minutes, or you had to guess at
something, that is a bug worth reporting.
